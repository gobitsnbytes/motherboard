# Motherboard Product Context

## Register

product

## Users

Motherboard is used by the bits&bytes™ network: roughly 100 people across 30 city forks. Operators, city leads, track leads, contributors, finance owners, and platform administrators use it while coordinating real work, membership, meetings, permissions, and money.

The primary job is to answer “what can I do, where, and why?” quickly, then let an authorized operator complete the task without guessing at hidden state.

## Product Purpose

Motherboard is the operational control plane for the network. It connects Discord-backed identity, city/fork membership, permissions, meetings, plugins, audit history, and finance workflows in one dependable workspace.

Success means people can operate across cities without accidentally seeing or changing the wrong city, and administrators can explain every access decision and mutation.

## Brand Personality

Direct, ambitious, technical — with a little controlled unruliness. The product should feel serious enough for governance and finance, but still carry the student-led energy of bits&bytes™.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative glassmorphism, excessive gradients, ornamental motion, meaningless metric cards, and dense admin screens that hide the reason an action is allowed or denied.

## Design Principles

- Make authorization legible: show effective access, scope, source, expiry, and audit trail.
- Prefer safe defaults: explicit scope, clear confirmation, reversible actions, and useful failure states.
- Keep the operator in flow: fast scanning, predictable navigation, inline edits, and strong keyboard support.
- Use brand energy as hierarchy and signal, not as decoration that competes with operational data.
- Design for the network: city boundaries and global roles must be visible wherever they matter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must be keyboard usable, retain visible focus, preserve readable contrast, support reduced motion, and avoid color-only meaning. Dense tables need responsive alternatives and clear empty, loading, forbidden, and error states.
