# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Motherboard is used by the GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION board, executive operators, fork leads, finance reviewers, legal operators, and technical administrators. Public guests use narrowly scoped booking, signing, and verification surfaces without entering the internal dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Motherboard is the operational system of record for the bits&bytes™ network. It coordinates identity, forks, compliance evidence, meetings, signatures, finance, and approved plugins without replacing statutory authorities, banks, certifying authorities, or professional legal and accounting review.

Success means every displayed state is backed by real source data, sensitive actions are authorized and audited, integrations fail closed, and a complete workflow can be reproduced from its evidence trail.

## Brand Personality

Direct, high-agency, and trustworthy. The product should feel like a serious operational instrument built by the bits&bytes™ team, not a generic enterprise dashboard or a playful community microsite.

## Anti-references

- Generic blue SaaS dashboards and glassmorphism.
- Decorative compliance scores without source evidence.
- Fake live metrics, seeded operational records, or optimistic success states.
- Excessive cards, pills, gradients, rounded surfaces, and tiny uppercase labels.
- Legal or financial claims that exceed what the stored evidence proves.

## Design Principles

1. Show source truth, provenance, and uncertainty.
2. Make the next operational action obvious without hiding controls or risk.
3. Use one consistent bits&bytes™ shell and component language across domains.
4. Preserve history: corrections are new events or reversals, never silent rewrites.
5. Public surfaces disclose only the minimum data needed to complete the task.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. All workflows must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, 44px touch targets, reduced motion, readable 16px body copy on mobile, and non-colour status indicators. Minor-facing and guardian-facing flows must use plain language and explicit consent.
