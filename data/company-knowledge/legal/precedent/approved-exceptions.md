# Precedent & Exception Policy

1. **Enterprise Counterparties**: Liability caps up to 24 months of fees may be accepted for Tier-1 enterprise counterparties with written approval from the Legal Lead or CFO.
2. **Payment Terms Extension**: Net 45 terms are allowable without escalation for municipal or institutional educational entities.
3. **Arbitration Venue**: International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) arbitration in Singapore is acceptable if counterparty refuses Indian courts.
