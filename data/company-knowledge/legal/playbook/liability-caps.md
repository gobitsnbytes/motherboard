# Rule: Liability Cap Ceiling
Liability cap must not exceed 12 months of total contract fees or $100,000 USD (whichever is lower), unless approved via the [exception process](/legal/precedent/approved-exceptions.md).

# Uncapped Indemnity Restriction
Uncapped indemnity or unlimited consequential damages clauses are strictly forbidden without board approval.
