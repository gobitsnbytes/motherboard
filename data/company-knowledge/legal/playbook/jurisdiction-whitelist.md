# Rule: Governing Law & Jurisdiction
Governing law must be specified as courts of Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, India or High Court of Judicature at Allahabad (Lucknow Bench). Foreign counterparties may specify Delaware USA, London UK, or Singapore.
