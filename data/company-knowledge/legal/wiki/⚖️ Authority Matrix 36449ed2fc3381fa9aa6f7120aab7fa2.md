> ⚖️ The Authority Matrix is the single source of truth for who can do what inside bits&bytes™ Foundation. If it's not in here, you don't have authority to do it. Authority flows only from written delegation — not from titles, history, or assumptions. **Full stop.**
# What This Document Is
The Authority Matrix is the Foundation's binding decision map. It specifies the exact scope of authority delegated to each role across every major decision domain.
This document is set, maintained, and can only be amended by the Board of Directors.
---
# How to Read This Matrix
Each section covers a decision domain. For every action, the matrix states what each role may do:
- ✅ **Full Authority** — This role can take this action independently, within any stated limits.
- 🔁 **Dual Auth Required** — Two named roles must both approve before the action can proceed. Neither alone is sufficient.
- 📋 **Recommend / Escalate** — This role may initiate, propose, or recommend — but cannot approve or execute without upstream sign-off.
- 🚫 **No Authority** — This role has no authority over this action, regardless of title, seniority, or past practice.
- ➖ **Not Applicable** — This action falls outside the scope of this role.
**If you are unsure whether you need Authority Matrix clearance — assume you do. Escalate before acting.**
---
# Roles Covered
> 📌 **Board of Directors (BoD)** — Constitutional and fiduciary authority. Highest governance body. All reserved matters sit here.
> **Chief Executive Officer (CEO)** — Executive leadership, organisational coordination, external strategy, operations, and co-stewardship.
> **Chief Technology Officer (CTO)** — Executive leadership across technology, systems, infrastructure, repositories, platform architecture, and co-stewardship.
> The CEO and CTO are exact co-equal executive leads. Neither outranks the other. Major decisions require both unless the Board has expressly delegated a narrower scope.
> **Chief Operating Officer (COO)** — Delegated authority over day-to-day operations, programmes, events, volunteer management, and Fork coordination.
> **Chief Financial Officer (CFO)** — Delegated authority over financial approvals, budget management, accounting, and financial compliance.
> **Fork Lead (FL)** — Local operational coordination within a recognised Fork. No binding authority over the Foundation.
> **Local Maintainer (LM)** — Support and maintenance functions within a Fork. No binding authority over the Foundation.
> **Volunteer / Contributor (Vol)** — Participates in Foundation activities. No governance or binding authority of any kind.

# 9. Reserved Matters (Board Only)
The following matters are **permanently reserved** to the Board of Directors and cannot be delegated under any circumstances:
- Amendment of the Memorandum of Association or Articles of Association
- Amendment of this Authority Matrix
- Admission or termination of Members
- Appointment or removal of Directors
- Winding up, dissolution, or amalgamation of the Foundation
- Any alteration to the Foundation's objects or Section 8 status
- Approval of any transaction creating a conflict of interest for a Director
- Any matter that would constitute a distribution of profits to Members
---
# 10. The Core Principle: No Implied Authority
> 🔴 **No title, badge, email address, public role, seniority, repository access, founder status, or historical action creates authority to act on behalf of bits&bytes™ Foundation.**
> This means:
> - Being called "CEO" or “COO”  does not give authority to sign contracts
> - Being a Fork Lead does not give authority to open bank accounts
> - Having done something before does not give authority to keep doing it
> - Being a Board Director does not automatically give individual spending authority beyond what this Matrix allows
> - Running a public event does not create implied authority over Foundation finances or partnerships
