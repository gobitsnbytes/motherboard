# 💰 Sponsorships & Financial Limits

Owner: Bits Bytes

## The central rule

All money, sponsorships, grants, donations, reimbursements, event fees, participation contributions, institutional support, in-kind support, and other economic benefits connected with the Foundation, the Network, or any Fork must be **accepted, collected, invoiced, receipted, recorded, and routed only through Foundation-approved financial systems**.

---

## Absolute prohibitions (org-wide)

Unless the Board gives **explicit written approval and supervision**, the following are prohibited for any Foundation-connected activity:

- Personal UPI collections
- Personal bank routing
- Local or informal bank accounts (including Fork accounts without approval)
- Cash collections
- Personal wallets or third-party pass-through accounts
- Unapproved payment links
- Informal sponsor collection
- Unapproved fundraising channels
- Personal custody of institutional funds
- Any account, wallet, QR code, or payment handle presented as a Foundation account without written approval

---

## No local financial independence

Forks do **not** have independent financial personality. All funds attributable to or connected with a Fork are treated as **Foundation-controlled funds** unless the Board has expressly approved a different lawful structure in writing.

---

## Banking

- Bank accounts, payment accounts, and digital payment facilities may be opened and operated only in the **name of the Foundation**
- Only under **Board authority**, the Authority Matrix, and Applicable Law
- Payments above Authority Matrix thresholds require **dual approval** — at least one of whom is a Director or designated senior officer

---

## Expense reimbursement

No person may incur, approve, or promise reimbursement unless:

- The expense is within an **approved budget** or event approval
- There is a **written delegation** or Authority Matrix limit
- Records are maintained (receipts, invoices, routing details)

Informal promises of reimbursement are **not valid**.

---

## Sponsorships, grants, and local receipts

All sponsorships, grants, donations, event income, and institutional funds connected with the Foundation or a Fork must be:

1. Accepted only through Company-approved financial systems
2. Documented (source, purpose, approval, routing, spend, reporting)
3. Not accepted on terms that conflict with the Foundation's mission, safety standards, brand rules, or legal status

---

## Annual budget

The Board approves an annual budget before or near the start of each Financial Year (1 April – 31 March). Material unbudgeted expenditure requires separate Board or Authority Matrix approval.

---

## Financial Year

1 April to 31 March.

---

## Records and audit

Every approved financial activity must have sufficient records for review:

- Source of funds and purpose
- Approval chain
- Invoice or receipt
- Routing channel
- Spend details and supporting documents
- Final reporting

The Board may appoint internal auditors, consultants, or other professionals to review accounts, controls, Fork finances, or compliance systems.

Forks can absolutely find local sponsors to fund venue space, food, or swags for events.

However, because all funds must be legally routed through the Foundation, all sponsorship conversations that involve money must loop in the Foundation before any commitment is made.

Sponsors give money to the legally registered Section 8 company, not to the local Fork directly. The Foundation will allocate those funds to your Fork's needs.

# Sponsorships & Financial Limits

## Can Forks get sponsors?

Yes — but only within the boundaries set by the Foundation. A Fork can approach, discuss, and mobilise local support. But the moment money changes hands, it must go through Foundation-approved financial systems. Not your personal account. Not a local UPI. Through the Foundation.

## The Golden Rule

**All money connected to bits&bytes™ — sponsorships, donations, event fees, grants, in-kind support — must be accepted, collected, invoiced, receipted, and routed only through Foundation-approved financial channels.**

No exceptions. No "just this once." No "the sponsor only wants to pay my UPI."

## What's absolutely prohibited

- ❌ Personal UPI collections (Paytm, GPay, PhonePe, etc.) for Fork or Foundation activity
- ❌ Personal bank routing
- ❌ Local or informal bank accounts opened in the Fork's name
- ❌ Cash collections outside of approved structures
- ❌ Unapproved payment links
- ❌ Informal sponsor arrangements made verbally or over chat
- ❌ Personal custody of funds meant for the Foundation or a Fork
- ❌ Accepting donor or sponsor terms that conflict with our mission or independence

## Who can sign a sponsorship agreement?

Not you, unless you've been expressly authorised in writing by the Board. This means:

- Fork Leads cannot sign sponsorship agreements
- Local Maintainers cannot sign sponsorship agreements
- Volunteers and participants cannot sign sponsorship agreements
- No one can sign just because they've done it before or because a sponsor is impatient

If a sponsor asks you to sign something: pause, and escalate upstream immediately.

## What Fork funds belong to

All funds attributable to or connected with a Fork — even if they were raised locally — belong to the Foundation. They are Foundation-controlled funds. Forks don't have independent financial personality.

This means local sponsorship money isn't "yours" to manage independently. It's the Foundation's, and it gets managed through the Foundation's systems.

## Expense approvals

You cannot spend money on behalf of the Foundation or a Fork unless the expense is:

- Within an approved budget
- Covered by an event approval
- Covered by a written delegation
- Within your Authority Matrix limit

Informal promises of reimbursement are not valid. Get it in writing before you spend.

<aside>
⚠️ Stop-and-escalate situations: If money is being offered, requested, collected, reimbursed, or held — pause and escalate. If a third party is asking for an invoice, a receipt, or a financial commitment — pause and escalate. Do not proceed.

</aside>
