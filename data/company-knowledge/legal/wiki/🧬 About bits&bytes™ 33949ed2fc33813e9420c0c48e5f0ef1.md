# What is bits&bytes™?

bits&bytes™ is a youth-led, builder-first technology education foundation. It exists to help young people — students, teenagers, first-time builders — actually build real things, not just watch tutorials and sit in classrooms.

We run hackathons. We ship products. We build communities. We run cohort programs, hacker houses, open-source teams, and local builder groups (called Forks) across cities. Every part of bits&bytes™ is designed to let you move fast, learn by doing, and own what you create.

## The mission

Enable young builders, contributors, organisers, mentors and communities to learn, build, collaborate and contribute through locally adaptive technology, education, open-source, community and public-benefit initiatives.

That's it. No fluff. The whole organization exists to serve that sentence.

## What we actually do

- Hackathons, build nights, hack nights, sprint sessions
- Cohort-based learning programs with competitive selection
- Hacker houses and residential builder residencies
- Forks — local builder communities in cities across India
- Open-source projects and digital public goods
- Mentorship, incubation, and venture support for student projects
- STEM education and digital literacy for underserved communities

## How the Foundation is structured

**GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION** (trading as bits&bytes™) is the upstream home for the Network. That means:

- Money stays in service of the mission
- The Board holds final governance authority
- Forks are local operating units, not separate organizations
- What gets built here is stewarded for the community

## Who's in the community?

Everyone who shows up. Builders. Designers. Open-source contributors. Fork Leads. Local Maintainers. Mentors. Alumni. Participants in cohorts. Organisers. Event volunteers. You.

Showing up does not make you a governing member — but it absolutely makes you part of bits&bytes™.

The bits&bytes™ name is an unregistered trademark (™) of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION.

## The vibe check

We are builder-first. We celebrate shipping, not just planning. Forks can have their own culture, events, and community personality, but the network stays one system with one standard for safety and integrity.

High agency. High integrity. Build things. Keep people safe.

## Rules of the road

Three documents govern how the Network operates. You don't need to memorise them, but you should know they exist.

- **Network Governance Charter** — How the Foundation and the Network relate to each other. What Forks are, what they aren't, who has authority, and how the brand works.
- **Internal Operating Manual** — How the day-to-day actually works. Financial controls, safeguarding rules, the Authority Matrix, and what to do when something goes wrong.
- [**Code of Conduct**](https://gobitsnbytes.org/coc) — Community standards. Applies to everyone in every bits&bytes™ space. Binding from the moment you participate.
