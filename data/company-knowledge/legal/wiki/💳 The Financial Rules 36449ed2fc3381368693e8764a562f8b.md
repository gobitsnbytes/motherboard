# 💳 The Financial Rules

Owner: Bits Bytes

## What counts as a breach

A breach means someone acted outside the system. That includes:

- unsafe conduct in a Foundation-connected setting
- handling money without written approval
- signing, promising, or accepting obligations without authority
- misusing the brand, name, logo, domain, or IP
- ignoring safeguarding concerns
- retaliating against someone who raised a concern
- misusing digital access, accounts, or credentials
- claiming authority that was never given
- creating a parallel identity or entity without Board approval
- any other move that creates avoidable legal, financial, safety, or reputation risk

---

## What the Foundation may do

The Foundation may respond with whatever is proportionate to the issue:

- give directions
- ask for training or records
- pause an event, project, or activity
- remove someone from a role
- revoke digital access or credentials
- freeze a project or programme
- suspend a Fork
- archive a Fork
- revoke Fork Recognition and brand permission
- require repayment or documentation of funds
- notify affected parties

---

## No waiver

Failure to enforce the governance framework in one situation does **not** waive the Foundation's right to enforce it later or in another situation.

---

## How this is meant to work

This page exists to protect young builders, keep the Foundation steady, and leave room for local creativity without losing control of the system.

---

## Good-faith escalation

If someone pauses an activity to check authority, refuses to collect money, declines to sign, reports misconduct, or asks for review, that person is protected. Escalation is part of the system, not a failure of it.

Fork Leads do not act as financial guarantors or money handlers for the Foundation.

## Hard no's

- do not sign contracts or agreements in the name of bits&bytes™
- do not route sponsorships, event fees, or any funds into a personal account or personal UPI
- do not create a separate entity using the bits&bytes™ name
- do not promise deliverables to partners without approval

If something is unclear, escalate it and let the Foundation handle it through the proper channel.

# The Financial Rules

Money stays inside the mission. No private benefit, no informal channels, no side accounts, no casual promises. If it touches bits&bytes™, it follows the system.

## The central rule

All money, sponsorships, grants, donations, reimbursements, event fees, participation contributions, institutional support, and in-kind support connected with bits&bytes™ or any Fork must go through Foundation-approved financial systems.

## Do / do not

**Do:**

- route incoming funds through Foundation-approved accounts and payment systems
- get written approval before spending on behalf of a Fork or the Foundation
- keep records of source, purpose, approval, invoice, and routing
- escalate immediately if someone asks you to handle money you are not authorised to handle
- ask before spending, even when it feels obvious

**Do not:**

- collect money through personal UPI, personal bank account, or personal wallet
- open local or informal accounts in the Fork's name or the Foundation's name
- accept cash and hold it yourself
- use unapproved payment links or QR codes for bits&bytes™ collections
- promise reimbursement informally
- personally hold institutional funds, sponsorship cheques, or event income
- route Foundation or Fork money through a third-party pass-through account
- open or operate any external account, handle, page, email alias, payment link, bank account, or wallet in the Fork's or Foundation's name unless it is centrally created and controlled by the Foundation, and all credentials remain Foundation property

## Forks do not handle money independently

A Fork is not a separate financial entity. It cannot run its own bank account, UPI, or treasury. All Fork-connected funds stay under Foundation control.

If a sponsor approaches a Fork directly, the money still goes through Foundation systems.

## Banking and payment channels

Bank accounts, payment accounts, digital payment facilities, and other financial arrangements may only be opened and operated in the name of bits&bytes™ Foundation, under Board authority and the Authority Matrix, with dual approval for payments above the threshold set in the Authority Matrix.

## Financial permissions are revocable

Any financial permission granted to a Fork or person is limited, revocable, documented, auditable, and can be paused or removed at any time for compliance, safeguarding, financial-control, or reputational reasons.
