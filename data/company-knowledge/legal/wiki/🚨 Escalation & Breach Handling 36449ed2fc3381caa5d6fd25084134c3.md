# Escalation & Breach Handling

This page covers what happens when something goes wrong inside bits&bytes™ — whether it's a financial violation, a safeguarding concern, a brand misuse, an authority breach, or a participant conduct issue. It also covers how the Foundation responds.

## When to escalate immediately

Stop what you're doing and escalate to the Foundation as soon as you encounter any of the following:

- Any safeguarding concern — harassment, bullying, misconduct, grooming, unsafe environment, any situation putting a minor at risk
- Money being moved, collected, or held outside Foundation-approved channels
- A third party asking for a signature, invoice, formal commitment, or financial guarantee
- Someone using the bits&bytes™ name or brand without authorisation
- Suspected fraud, misrepresentation, or financial irregularity
- Conflicts of interest that haven't been disclosed
- A situation where you're uncertain whether you have authority to proceed
- Any action that could bind the Foundation legally or financially without Board-level authorisation

## Types of breach

Breaches vary in severity but all require a response:

- **Financial breach** — informal fund collection, unauthorised spend, personal account use, cash handling, unapproved payment links
- **Authority breach** — signing agreements, making representations, or taking actions without written delegated authority
- **Safeguarding breach** — failure to meet safeguarding standards, failure to report, retaliation against a reporter, creation of unsafe environments
- **Brand breach** — unauthorised use of the bits&bytes™ name, logo, domains, or identity
- **Mission breach** — activity that is illegal, harmful, discriminatory, or inconsistent with the Foundation's not-for-profit character
- **Conduct breach** — harassment, bullying, discrimination, intimidation, misuse of authority over community members

## How the Foundation responds

When a breach is identified, the Foundation may take any combination of the following actions:

1. **Immediate suspension** — of any activity, event, digital space, or Fork access that poses ongoing risk. No prior notice required.
2. **Investigation** — fact-finding to understand what happened, who was involved, and what the impact was.
3. **Remediation** — corrective action such as repayment of funds, policy reinforcement, retraining, or process changes.
4. **Disciplinary action** — warnings, removal from role, suspension from the network, permanent exclusion.
5. **Fork consequences** — probation, suspension, archiving, or revocation of Fork recognition.
6. **Escalation to authorities** — in serious cases involving fraud, safeguarding failures, or criminal conduct, the Foundation will escalate to relevant legal or regulatory bodies.

## Protecting people who report

Anyone who raises a genuine concern in good faith is protected. The Foundation will not penalise, exclude, isolate, or retaliate against someone for reporting a concern — even if, after investigation, the concern turns out to be unfounded.

Retaliation against reporters is itself a breach. It will be treated as seriously as the original concern.

## Emergency powers

In situations where waiting for a formal process would cause harm, the Board or an authorised delegate may act immediately to:

- Suspend access to digital systems, platforms, accounts, and spaces
- Freeze any financial activity connected to a Fork or individual
- Revoke brand usage, Fork recognition, or operational permissions
- Remove an individual from community spaces pending investigation

These emergency actions are not punishments — they're protective. A full process follows.

## The right instinct

If something feels wrong: escalate. Don't handle it informally. Don't try to fix it yourself without telling anyone. Don't stay silent because you're worried about causing trouble.

You won't be in trouble for raising concerns. You might be in trouble for not raising them.
