# 🛡️ Safeguarding & Participant Safety

Owner: Bits Bytes

## What safeguarding means here

Safeguarding, participant wellbeing, child protection, harassment prevention, anti-discrimination, lawful consent, supervision, event safety, online safety, digital platform governance, reporting channels, and incident escalation are **core governance priorities** of the Foundation — not optional compliance items.

The Foundation is explicitly and predominantly youth-led. This makes safeguarding obligations more significant, not less.

---

## Universal application

Every Fork, event, cohort, digital community, repository, group chat, workshop, school partnership, mentorship arrangement, public programme, and local initiative must comply with the Foundation's safeguarding and participant wellbeing standards.

---

## Required standards

The Foundation may adopt and enforce:

- [Codes of conduct](http://gobitsnbytes.org/coc)
- Consent requirements
- Supervision standards and volunteer standards
- Access restrictions and moderation procedures
- Reporting procedures and inquiry processes
- Emergency protocols and escalation procedures
- Remedial measures proportionate to the nature of the activity

---

## Child protection

Where minors are involved, relevant teams must follow requirements on:

- **Consent** — parent or guardian consent where required
- **Supervision** — appropriate adult oversight
- **Communication** — no unsupervised direct contact with minors by adults
- **Photography and recording** — explicit consent required
- **Data and platform access** — age-appropriate restrictions
- **Travel and residential programs** — enhanced safeguarding for residential and travel contexts

No team may dilute these requirements for convenience.

---

## Anti-harassment and anti-discrimination

Prohibited in all Foundation-connected settings, offline and online:

- Harassment, bullying, intimidation, stalking, grooming, coercion
- Sexual misconduct
- Discriminatory conduct or exclusionary targeting
- Hostile online behaviour
- Retaliation against anyone raising a concern
- Misuse of authority

---

## Online communities

Digital communities must be moderated to protect minors and young contributors:

- Access controls and role limits
- Content removal, warning systems, temporary and permanent restrictions
- Escalation to adults and platform-admin review
- Preservation of incident records

---

## Reporting and escalation

Every Fork and digital community must maintain a **clear reporting path** for:

- Safety and misconduct
- Harassment and discrimination
- Financial irregularity
- Data misuse and platform abuse
- Other material concerns

Reports must be escalated promptly to the person or body specified by the Board or applicable policy.

---

## Records and confidentiality

Safeguarding reports and incident records must be:

- Handled with care, confidentiality, and appropriate access limits
- Shared only with persons who need them for safety, inquiry, compliance, support, escalation, or remedial action

---

## No retaliation

Retaliation against any person who raises a concern, reports misconduct, supports a participant, refuses unsafe conduct, or escalates a safeguarding matter is **prohibited**.

---

## Emergency and suspension powers

The Board or an authorised delegate may **immediately suspend** any event, digital space, access right, volunteer role, Fork permission, leadership role, repository access, communication channel, or public-facing activity if necessary to prevent legal, financial, physical, emotional, online, or reputational harm.

Safeguarding is our number one priority. We are a youth-led network, meaning there is an extra burden of responsibility to keep our members safe from harm, exploitation, or harassment.

If there is a child protection concern, serious misconduct, or safeguarding issue, you must escalate it immediately to hello@gobitsnbytes.org. You do not have to handle severe issues alone.

Safeguarding policies override any concept of 'local autonomy'.

# Safeguarding & Participant Safety

Because bits&bytes™ is a youth-led network where many participants are minors, safeguarding is a core governance priority. It's not optional, it's not negotiable, and it applies to every Fork, every event, every digital space, and every cohort.

## What safeguarding covers

- Child protection and participant wellbeing
- Anti-harassment, anti-bullying, anti-discrimination
- Lawful consent requirements (especially where minors are involved)
- Event safety and supervision standards
- Online safety and digital platform governance
- Data protection and privacy for participants
- Incident reporting and escalation

## Universal application — no exceptions

Every Fork, event, cohort, digital community, repository, group chat, workshop, school partnership, mentorship arrangement, public programme, and local initiative must comply with the Foundation's safeguarding standards.

No Fork can water down these requirements for convenience. No Fork can skip consent forms because things "move fast." These standards exist to protect everyone — including you.

## Prohibited conduct

The following are prohibited in all Foundation-connected settings — offline and online:

- Harassment, bullying, intimidation, stalking, grooming, coercion
- Sexual misconduct
- Discriminatory conduct or exclusionary targeting
- Hostile online behaviour and misuse of authority
- Retaliation against someone who raised a concern

## Online moderation

Digital communities must be moderated to protect minors and young contributors. This includes access controls, role limits, content removal, warning systems, and escalation to adults when needed.

If you're running a Discord, WhatsApp, or any other community channel for your Fork: you're responsible for setting and enforcing moderation standards.

## Reporting & escalation

Every Fork and digital community must maintain a clear path for reporting safety concerns. If you receive a report:

1. Take it seriously immediately
2. Escalate promptly to the person or body specified by the Foundation
3. Preserve records with appropriate confidentiality
4. Do not retaliate against or punish the person who reported

## Emergency powers

The Board or an authorised delegate can immediately suspend an event, digital space, access right, volunteer role, Fork permission, or any activity if necessary to prevent harm. No prior notice required if safety is at stake.

<aside>
💡 Remember: Safeguarding requirements exist to protect normal youth-led participation, not to convert it into unnecessary bureaucracy. If you're unsure about something — just ask upstream.

</aside>
