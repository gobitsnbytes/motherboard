Dear [Fork Lead Name],
This notice is issued on behalf of the Executive Office of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION to formally notify you that the Fork Recognition granted to your local team has been placed in Archival Dormancy.
Pursuant to Section 2.3 of the Network Governance Charter (NGC v2.0) and Section 7.3 of the Internal Operating Manual (IOM v2.0), this action is taken due to prolonged inactivity, lack of monthly Pulse Updates, or a vacancy in local leadership.
1. OPERATIONAL CONSEQUENCES OF ARCHIVAL DORMANCY
Effective immediately, the following operational changes apply to your Fork:
• Brand Rights Paused: The permission to utilize the bits&bytes™ name, logo, 3D cube mark, and marketing materials for local events is suspended;
• Digital Assets Paused: Any associated subdomains (e.g. city.gobitsnbytes.org) will be redirected or deactivated, and local Discord servers will be placed in read-only archiving;
• Records Handover: The Fork Lead must immediately transfer all local records, contributor directories, and project materials to the Head of Operations;
• External Representation: The Fork and its members shall not represent themselves as active, recognised units of the bits&bytes™ Network.
2. STEPS REQUIRED FOR REVIVAL
Archival Dormancy is a protective state, not a permanent termination. Recognition may be revived and restored to active status if the local team satisfies the following conditions:
1. Appointing and onboarding a qualified Fork Lead who successfully completes the Board interview process;
2. Submitting a clear, 3-month activity roadmap detailing planned cohorts, events, and builder meetups;
3. Executing a fresh Fork Recognition Agreement (Form No. 4) and establishing a monthly reporting cadence.
If no steps are taken to revive the Fork within ninety (90) calendar days of this notice, the Fork Recognition will be permanently revoked, and the Fork will be closed.
We appreciate your past efforts in building the local community and hope to see your Fork active again in the future.
