2. IMMEDIATE DEACTIVATION OF ACCESS
The Chief Technology Officer has deactivated your access credentials across all Foundation infrastructure. You are strictly barred from logging into or interacting with:

• System repositories and design files (GitHub organizations, Figma teams, databases, website servers);

During this suspension, you must strictly comply with the following instructions:
1. DO NOT represent yourself as an active volunteer, lead, or agent of the Fork or Foundation;
2. DO NOT delete, modify, or transfer any local records, communications, database entries, or code files related to bits&bytes™; you are commanded to preserve all such evidence;
3. Cooperate fully with the Board or its designated investigators if contacted for a statement.

The internal review will be completed as expeditiously as possible. You will be contacted in writing once the review is concluded, detailing whether you will be reactivated, transitioned, or formally disassociated. Direct any inquiry regarding this status to hello@gobitsnbytes.org.
