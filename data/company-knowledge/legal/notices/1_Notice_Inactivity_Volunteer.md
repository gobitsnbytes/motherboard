1. CONTEXT & GOVERNING PRINCIPLES
At bits&bytes™, our organization is built upon a builder-first, high-agency model. As articulated in Section 6.1 of our Internal Operating Manual (IOM v2.0) and our team guidelines, we default to pairing, sharing context, and, most importantly, SHIPPING REAL PRODUCTS. High agency and resilience are the core principles that sustain our youth-led developer network. Continued inactivity without communication places an undue burden on your fellow team members and hinders the operations of your attached Fork/Team.
2. POLICY & RULES REFERENCE
Please be advised of the following provisions of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION's governance framework, which you agreed to when executing your Volunteer & Engagement Form (Form No. 1):
• Section 4.5 & 4.6 (IOM v2.0): Access and privileges (including GitHub organization membership, Slack/Discord channels, and internal resources) are delegated operational permissions subject to active participation and compliance.
• Section 7.2(a) (IOM v2.0): Fork Leads and Maintainers are required to maintain active rosters and report on the operational logs of all active volunteers to Upstream.
• Section 9.1 (IOM v2.0): The Foundation reserves the right to take corrective action, including the temporary suspension of roles, access privileges, or formal disassociation from the Network for inactivity or non-compliance.
• Section F, Clause 4 (Volunteer & Engagement Form): Your voluntary engagement may be suspended or terminated by the Foundation at any time in accordance with its governance framework.
• Section F, Clause 9 (Volunteer & Engagement Form): There is no minimum weekly time requirement, and you are free to step back, but we require reasonable notice and open communication to ensure smooth handovers.

3. REQUIRED ACTION & RESOLUTION
To resolve your volunteer status, you are requested to choose one of the following paths within seventy-two (72) hours of the receipt of this notice (by [Deadline Date and Time]):
PATH A -- REACTIVATION & ENGAGEMENT
☐ You wish to remain an active volunteer. To choose this path, you must: (1) Respond to this notice via email or official Discord direct message to the CCO/COO (Aadrika Maurya) or Head of Operations (Raghwender Vasisth) explaining your recent absence; (2) Outline a concrete plan to resume your contributions, including availability hours; and (3) Schedule a brief status sync with your Lead to realign on pending tasks.
PATH B -- GRACEFUL TRANSITION (ALUMNI STATUS)
☐ You wish to step down due to academic commitments, personal constraints, or other priorities. To choose this path, you must: (1) Coordinate with your Fork Lead or Team Lead to hand over any pending tasks, access credentials, code repositories, or materials; and (2) Formally reply requesting a transition to "Alumni" status, allowing us to update the active rosters.
4. CONSEQUENCES OF NON-RESPONSE
If we do not receive any communication from you by the deadline of [Deadline Date and Time], GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION will proceed with the following actions:
1. Automatic disassociation of your status from the active volunteer roster;
2. Revocation of access to official communication channels (Discord role privileges, Slack/Discord workspaces);
3. Removal of permissions/membership from official GitHub organizations, Figma teams, and other digital infrastructure;
4. Deactivation of any assigned bits&bytes™ email aliases or directories.
We deeply value the effort, passion, and time you have contributed to the bits&bytes™ Network. We understand that personal, professional, or academic demands arise, and our priority is to maintain clarity and operational health across our forks and nodes.
