Dear [Volunteer Name],
This notice is issued on behalf of the Executive Office of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION to formally acknowledge and confirm your voluntary resignation from your role within the bits&bytes™ Network.
Pursuant to Section F, Clause 9 of your Volunteer & Engagement Form (Form No. 1) and Section 7.4 of the Internal Operating Manual (IOM v2.0), we acknowledge your decision to step down from your role as [Assigned Role], effective on [Effective Exit Date].
1. TRANSITION & HANDOVER OBLIGATIONS
To ensure a professional and orderly transition, you are required to complete the following offboarding tasks prior to your effective exit date:
1. Handover all local files, project documentation, contact directories, and activity logs to your direct domain Lead or the Head of Operations;
2. Relinquish all administrative access, passwords, and credentials for any local Fork channels, email accounts, or shared folders;
3. Return or transfer ownership of all digital assets and repositories (including code, design assets, and event files) created in connection with your bits&bytes™ activities to the Foundation.
2. STATUS OF SYSTEM ACCESS
As of the Effective Exit Date, the following administrative updates will occur:
• Discord/Slack: Active volunteer roles and access to developer-only or coordinator-only channels will be deactivated.
• GitHub/Figma: Membership in the official GOBITSNBYTES GitHub organization and Figma workspaces will be set to inactive or removed.
• Email Alias: Any assigned bits&bytes™ email alias or directory account will be deactivated.
3. TRANSITION TO ALUMNI STATUS
Subject to the satisfactory completion of your transition duties and a review of your contribution history, you may be eligible to transition to the "Alumni" roster. Alumni retain recognition for their past contributions and may be invited to participate in network-wide events or act as mentors. If offboarding is incomplete or in case of code of conduct issues, the relationship will be terminated as a formal disassociation.
4. LIMITATION OF REPRESENTATION
Following your exit, you shall have no authority to represent, contract for, speak on behalf of, or otherwise bind GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION or any bits&bytes™ Fork. Any public-facing representation of active status is strictly prohibited.
We thank you for the time, energy, and builder spirit you invested in bits&bytes™. We wish you the absolute best in your future endeavors.
