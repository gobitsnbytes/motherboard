NOTICE: THIS IS A FORMAL LEGAL CEASE AND DESIST NOTICE AND MANDATORY ASSET HANDOVER DEMAND.

Following the formal dissolution of the Prayagraj Fork by the Board of Directors of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION (the "Foundation") due to repeated material breaches of the bits&bytes™ Code of Conduct, Internal Operating Manual (IOM v2.0), and Network Governance Charter (NGC v2.0), you are hereby commanded to immediately cease and desist from using all Foundation brand assets and transfer all digital credentials.

1. CEASE AND DESIST MANDATE
Effective immediately, all former members, coordinators, and maintainers of the dissolved Prayagraj Fork must:
1. Cease and Desist from all use of the name "bits&bytes", "gobitsnbytes", associated logos, trademarks, 3D cube monogram, designs, and brand goodwill;
2. Immediately cease representing your local group, events, online channels, or projects as being affiliated with, recognized by, or in any way connected to GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION or the bits&bytes™ Network;
3. Immediately stop operating any parallel or breakaway entity claiming continuity, succession, or affiliation with the bits&bytes™ Network.

2. MANDATORY INSTAGRAM ACCOUNT HANDOVER
Pursuant to Section 2.5(b) of the Network Governance Charter (NGC v2.0) and Section 4.5 of the Internal Operating Manual (IOM v2.0), all digital assets and social media accounts operated under the bits&bytes™ brand belong solely to the Foundation.
Specifically, Ajitesh and any other former members holding administrative access, credentials, or recovery options for the Instagram account "@gobitsnbytes.prayagraj" are commanded to:
1. Hand over complete, exclusive administrative access (including username, current password, and transferring the recovery email and phone number to the Foundation's official credentials) to:
   • Aadrika Maurya (Chief Creative Officer & Chief Operating Officer)
   • Akshat Kushwaha (Chief Technology Officer)
2. Complete this transfer within twenty-four (24) hours of the transmission of this notice.

3. LEGAL BASIS & CODE OF CONDUCT VIOLATIONS
The dissolution of the Prayagraj Fork and this subsequent demand are based on the following documented violations:
• Material Disruption of Official Proceedings (Code of Conduct v1.0 Section 3.2): Repeated interruptions, jokes, and disrespectful responses ("aapse thodi na koi bol raha hai") during professional headquarters recruitment meetings.
• Insubordination & Hostile Communication (Code of Conduct v1.0 Section 3.2 - Harassment & Bullying): Rude and abusive language used to challenge Headquarters' operations, demanding changes rather than requesting them, and disputing the established governance structure (violating NGC v2.0 Section 1.5 - Upstream Stewardship).
• Violation of Professional Standards & Conduct (Code of Conduct v1.0 Section 3.2 - Disruption): Reacting to official organizational messages with inappropriate emojis ("pregnant man") and continuing to mock organizational disputes on public/semi-public Discord channels.

4. WARNING OF LEGAL ACTION
Failure to comply with the cease and desist mandate, or withholding/refusing to hand over the Instagram account "@gobitsnbytes.prayagraj" or any other digital property, will result in the immediate initiation of legal proceedings under Indian civil and criminal laws (including the Information Technology Act, 2000, and the Trade Marks Act, 1999) for brand infringement, unauthorized use of intellectual property, misappropriation of digital assets, and breach of binding governance documents.

All communications regarding this transition must be directed to hello@gobitsnbytes.org.
