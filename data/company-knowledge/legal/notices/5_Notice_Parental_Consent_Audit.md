Dear [Minor Name] & Parent/Guardian,
This notice is issued to verify the parental consent records of minor participants in the bits&bytes™ Network.
Pursuant to GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION's Safeguarding & Participant Safety Policy and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP), the processing of personal data of any participant under 18 years of age requires verifiable consent from their parent or legal guardian.
To ensure complete compliance and protect minor welfare, we are conducting a mandatory audit of all active minor organizers, Fork Leads, and contributors. Our records show that you are registered as under 18 and we require formal verification of your Parental / Guardian Consent Form (Form No. 2).
1. LEGAL CONTEXT (DPDP ACT 2023 & POCSO 2012)
Under Section 9 of the DPDP Act, a data fiduciary must obtain verifiable consent from the parent or lawful guardian before processing any personal data of a child. Additionally, POCSO 2012 guidelines require rigorous safeguarding of minors across all offline and online developer environments. Verifying identity and adult status is a statutory necessity.
2. MANDATORY ACTION REQUIRED
The parent or legal guardian who signed your Parental / Guardian Consent Form (Form No. 2) must complete verification within seven (7) calendar days of this notice, by [Verification Deadline]. Verification must be completed through one of the following approved methods:
☐ Option A: Phone/WhatsApp Call. The parent/guardian must schedule a brief 2-minute video callback or telephone verification with the Office of the CCO/COO to confirm their identity and consent.
☐ Option B: OTP Verification. The parent/guardian must reply to our verification SMS/WhatsApp message with the generated One-Time Password (OTP) sent to their registered phone number.
☐ Option C: Government ID Submission. Submitting a scanned copy or clear photograph of a government-issued identity document (e.g., Aadhaar card, Passport, Voter ID) verifying the parent's name and adult status to hello@gobitsnbytes.org.
3. CONSEQUENCES OF NON-COMPLIANCE
If verification is not completed by the deadline of [Verification Deadline]:
• Your active role and title within the bits&bytes™ Network will be temporarily paused;
• Access to official Discord servers, GitHub organizations, and operational portals will be suspended.
These credentials will be restored immediately upon completion of verification. We appreciate your cooperation in keeping bits&bytes™ a safe, legal, and compliant space for young builders.
