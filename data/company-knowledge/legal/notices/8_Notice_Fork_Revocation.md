Dear [Former Fork Lead Name],
NOTICE: THIS IS A FORMAL NOTICE OF REVOCATION AND DISAFFILIATION. GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION has terminated the Fork Recognition Agreement and revoked the Recognition of your Fork, effective immediately.
This action has been taken under Section 2.5 of the Network Governance Charter (NGC v2.0), Section 7.3 & 9.1(c) of the Internal Operating Manual (IOM v2.0), and Section 6.1 of the Fork Agreement due to a material breach of the agreement, safeguarding violations, or persistent non-compliance.
1. CEASE AND DESIST MANDATE
Effective immediately, the former Fork Lead, Local Maintainers, and members must:
1. Cease and Desist from all use of the bits&bytes™ name, brand, logo, trademark, 3D cube monogram, and goodwill;
2. Immediately stop presenting your local group, events, channels, or presentations as being recognized by or affiliated with GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION or the bits&bytes™ Network.
2. MANDATORY ASSET & CREDENTIAL TURNOVER
You are commanded to transfer all digital assets and credentials to GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION within twenty-four (24) hours of this notice:
• Technical Assets: Ownership and admin rights of all local code repositories (GitHub), design files (Figma), and databases must be transferred to the CTO (Akshat Kushwaha);
• Social Media & Communication: All credentials and ownership of local Discord servers, WhatsApp/Telegram groups, and handles must be handed over to the CCO/COO (Aadrika Maurya);
• Local Records: Handover all local participant records, directories, and sponsor histories to the Head of Operations.
3. DEACTIVATION OF SYSTEMS ACCESS
All active system accesses (GitHub organization memberships, Discord roles, and bits&bytes email aliases) associated with your Fork have been revoked by the CTO.
4. WARNING OF LEGAL CONSEQUENCES
Failure to comply with the cease and desist mandate, or withholding Foundation property, data, or credentials will result in immediate disassociation and the initiation of legal proceedings under Indian civil and criminal law for brand infringement, misappropriation of assets, and breach of contract.
This revocation is final. For any transition questions, contact hello@gobitsnbytes.org.
