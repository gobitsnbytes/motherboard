# Approved Standard Payment Terms Clause

```text
Company shall invoice Customer for all fees in accordance with the Schedule of Fees. All undisputed invoices shall be due and payable within thirty (30) days from the date of invoice issuance ("Net 30"). Payments shall be remitted via wire transfer or electronic ACH to the bank account designated in writing by Company.
```
