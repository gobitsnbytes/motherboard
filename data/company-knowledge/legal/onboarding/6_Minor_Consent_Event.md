SECTION D -- CONSENT, RELEASE & DECLARATION
I / We, being the parent(s) / guardian(s) of the above-named minor, hereby state and consent as follows:
1. PARTICIPATION: I / We give our informed consent for my / our ward to participate in the events, hackathons, workshops, and cohort programs organized by GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION. I / We understand that their participation is voluntary and subject to the bits&bytes™ Code of Conduct (gobitsnbytes.org/coc) and all safety rules.
2. TRAVEL: I / We give permission for my / our ward to travel to and from the event venue. I / We understand that GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION is not responsible for organizing daily transport unless explicitly specified in writing.
3. RELEASE & INDEMNITY: To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, I / We hereby release, waive, and discharge GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION, its directors, officers, employees, volunteers, and agents from any and all liability, claims, or demands for personal injury, illness, death, or property damage/loss, arising out of or in connection with my / our ward's attendance or participation in the event, except in cases of proven gross negligence.
4. EMERGENCY MEDICAL: In the event of a medical emergency, injury, or sudden illness where I / we cannot be contacted immediately, I / we hereby authorize GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION representatives to act as temporary guardians, to obtain medical treatment, and to consent to any medical or surgical decisions on behalf of my / our ward as advised by a licensed medical practitioner. I / we agree to bear all expenses associated with such medical care.
5. DPDP ACT 2023 COMPLIANCE: The Foundation may process the personal data collected in this form -- including my / our ward's name, age, and my own contact details as parent / guardian -- solely for the purposes of event registration, verification, safety, communication, and legal compliance. In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the Foundation will not use child data for targeted advertising, behavioral profiling, or tracking, and will keep the data only for the duration required by law.
6. VERIFICATION: I / We agree that the Foundation may contact me / us by phone, WhatsApp, or email, and may request a copy of a government-issued identity document to verify our identity and consent.
7. TRUTHFULNESS: I / We confirm that all information provided in this form is true, complete, and accurate, matching our official identity records.
