SECTION F -- CONSENT & DECLARATION
I / We, being the parent(s) / guardian(s) of the above-named minor, hereby state and consent as follows:
1. I / We give our informed consent for my / our ward to participate as a Fork Lead / volunteer / event participant / contributor in the activities of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION, subject to the Foundation's Code of Conduct (gobitsnbytes.org/coc), Safeguarding & Participant Safety Policy, Fork Handbook, and all Network Policies as published from time to time.
2. I / We understand and agree that the minor's role is entirely operational and participatory in nature and does not confer upon them any legal agency, directorship, signing authority, banking authority, or authority to bind GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION in any manner whatsoever. The minor may not sign contracts, collect or hold money, or make legal representations on behalf of the Foundation or any Fork.
3. I / We acknowledge that GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION operates under a comprehensive safeguarding framework and that the Board or its authorised officers retain supervisory authority over all Fork and Network operations. The Foundation's safeguarding standards comply with the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 (POCSO) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
4. I / We agree to ensure that my / our ward complies with all applicable policies and Network Policies of the Foundation at all times, and that I / we will notify the Foundation promptly if circumstances change that may affect this consent.
5. I / We accept that the Foundation may revoke the minor's Fork Recognition or role at any time in accordance with its governance framework.
6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY: To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, I/We consent to, and where I/We have authority to do so hereby assign and transfer to GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION, all transferable present and future rights in any code, design, content, documentation, artwork, event material, or other work product created by my/our ward in connection with the bits&bytes™ Network. If any part of that assignment is ineffective in law, I/We grant the Foundation an exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free licence to use, reproduce, adapt, modify, publish, distribute, display, communicate, translate, sublicense, and otherwise exploit such work product, and I/We shall sign any further document reasonably required to perfect the Foundation's rights when my/our ward attains majority. The minor will be credited for contributions where practicable. This does not cover IP created entirely outside Network participation.
7. DATA PROTECTION AND CHILD CONSENT: The Foundation may process the personal data of my/our ward and my/our own personal data only for onboarding, safeguarding, governance, coordination, communication, reporting, and lawful compliance, on the basis of clear notice and specific consent. Before any personal data of my/our ward is processed, the Foundation will verify my/our identity and adult status through an approved method. The Foundation will not use child data for targeted advertising, profiling, behavioural monitoring, or any tracking purpose, and will process only the minimum data necessary for the stated purposes.
8. DATA PROTECTION: The personal data collected in this form - including my / our ward's information and my / our own as parent / guardian - will be used solely for safeguarding, governance, coordination, and communication purposes by GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION, in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. The Foundation will not use my / our ward's personal data for targeted advertising, behavioural profiling, or tracking beyond what is reasonably necessary for safety and running Network programmes, and will not sell or otherwise monetise their personal data. Data will be retained for the duration of my / our ward's engagement and thereafter only as required by law. I / We may request deletion of personal data after engagement ends by writing to hello@gobitsnbytes.org.
9. I / We confirm that all information provided in this form is true and accurate.
10. VERIFICATION: I / We agree that the Foundation may contact me / us by phone, WhatsApp, or email, and may request an OTP confirmation or a copy of a government‑issued identity document, to verify my / our identity and this parental / guardian consent before or during processing of my / our ward's personal data.
