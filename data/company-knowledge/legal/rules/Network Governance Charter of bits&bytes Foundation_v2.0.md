# Network Governance Charter of bits&bytes Foundation_v2.0

Network Governance Charter of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION
Version 2.0 | Effective Date: 4 June 2026 | Approved by the Board of Directors
Version: 2.0 | Effective Date: 4 June 2026 | Approved by: Board of Directors
1. Preamble and Mission
1.1 Purpose of this Charter. This Network Governance Charter ("Charter") is the constitutional operating instrument for the bits&bytes Network (the "Network"). It establishes the structural relationship between the legal entity, the brand, the Board of Directors, the youth-led executive team, and the distributed community of local operational units. Its primary purpose is to steward a distributed, youth-led, builder-first ecosystem while preserving one coherent mission, one shared identity, one safeguarding standard, and one upstream governance layer.
1.2 Legal Entity vs. Brand Name.
(a) Legal Entity: The formal, registered legal entity is GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION (referred to herein as the "Company" or the "Foundation"), a non-profit company incorporated under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013 (limited by guarantee and not having share capital), in the State of Uttar Pradesh, India. All contracts, banking transactions, regulatory filings, asset ownership, and legal liabilities are held solely by GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION.
(b) Brand Name: "bits&bytes" (or "bitsnbytes" in contexts where the ampersand character is unavailable, unsupported, or restricted) is the public-facing brand name, community identity, and network descriptor. "bits&bytes" (or "bitsnbytes") is not a separate legal entity, partnership, or association. The right to use the "bits&bytes" (or "bitsnbytes") name, logo, design system, and digital assets is licensed and controlled exclusively by GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION.
1.3 Constitutional Basis. This Charter is adopted by the Board of Directors of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION pursuant to the powers vested in the Board under the Company's e-Articles of Association (e-AOA) - specifically Articles 19 and 23 (Proceedings of the Board and delegation to committees/representatives). This Charter is subordinate to the e-Memorandum of Association (e-MOA) and the e-AOA of the Company. If any provision of this Charter conflicts with the e-MOA or e-AOA, the provisions of the e-MOA and e-AOA shall prevail.
1.4 Network Mission and Scope. The Foundation exists to enable young builders, contributors, organisers, mentors, and communities to learn, build, collaborate, and contribute through locally adaptive technology, education, open-source, community, and public-benefit initiatives. The Network is designed to be high-agency and locally creative, operating within the boundaries of the 18 specific objects (A to R) set forth in Clause 3(a) of the Company's e-MOA, including but not limited to STEM education, youth empowerment, technology hackathons, cohort-based learning, and distributed learning networks (Forks and Nodes).
1.5 Upstream Stewardship. GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION, acting through its Board of Directors and its duly authorised officers, is the upstream legal and constitutional steward of the Network ("Upstream"). Upstream stewardship includes absolute responsibility for:
(a) Mission continuity and alignment;
(b) Safeguarding, child protection, and participant wellbeing;
(c) Intellectual property stewardship, brand protection, and trademark enforcement;
(d) Financial oversight, regulatory compliance, tax compliance, and auditing;
(e) The formal granting, suspension, archiving, and revocation of Fork Recognition;
(f) Digital infrastructure governance, domain management, and data protection.
1.6 Local Autonomy within Upstream Coherence. Local operational units ("Forks") are intended to be locally adaptive. Subject to this Charter, the Internal Operating Manual (IOM), and Board-approved policies, each Fork may adapt its local programming, culture, event formats, and learning activities to meet local community needs. This autonomy is strictly operational, creative, and participatory. It does not constitute legal independence, authority to contract, or ownership of the brand.
2. The Fork Network Model
2.1 Core Abstraction. The Foundation stewards a distributed network of locally autonomous units. The Network operates on the concept of "Forks" and "Nodes" as defined in Article I of the e-AOA:
(a) "Fork" means an official city-level, locality-level, institutional, digital, or thematic operational unit of the bits&bytes Network recognised by or under authority of the Board solely as an operational and participatory unit of the Company's mission. A Fork inherits the Company's mission, standards, safeguarding framework, brand identity, and applicable policies.
(b) "Node" means a broad technical or network descriptor for a point of activity, participation, infrastructure, or contribution within the Network. A Node does not denote an official local unit or confer Fork Recognition unless formally recognised as a Fork by the Board.
2.2 Nature of Fork Recognition. "Fork Recognition" is a limited, conditional, revocable, and non-exclusive operational and brand-use permission granted by the Board.
(a) Fork Recognition does not create a franchise, partnership, agency, joint venture, society, association, branch office, or subsidiary relationship.
(b) A Fork has no independent legal personality, no capacity to hold property, and no inherent authority to bind or represent GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION.
(c) Fork Recognition is granted, suspended, archived, revived, or revoked in accordance with the Board-approved Recognition Framework or Fork Protocols.
2.3 Fork Archive and Dormancy. "Fork Archive" means the temporary or continuing archival dormancy of a Fork without dissolving the possibility of later Fork Revival. A Fork may be archived for inactivity, leadership transitions, compliance lapses, safeguarding concerns, or at the request of the local team. Upon archiving, all brand permissions and digital privileges are suspended.
2.4 Fork Revival. "Fork Revival" means the formal restoration of an archived or suspended Fork. Revival requires the approval of the Board or its delegate, a designated Fork Lead, a compliance review, and the formal re-acceptance of this Charter and the IOM.
2.5 Effect of Revocation. If Fork Recognition is revoked:
(a) The local group must immediately cease all use of the name "bits&bytes", "gobitsnbytes", associated logos, domains, and marks.
(b) The group must hand over all digital assets, social media handles, code repositories, participant databases, and financial records to GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION.
(c) No group may operate a parallel or breakaway entity claiming continuity, succession, or affiliation with the bits&bytes Network without express written Board consent.
3. Separation of Powers and Governance Structure
3.1 Constitutional Governance (Upstream). Ultimate governance, legal, and financial authority is vested in the Board of Directors of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION. In accordance with the e-AOA, the Board is responsible for:
(a) Upholding the non-profit mandate (e-MOA Clause 5: all income applied solely to objects, no dividends);
(b) Statutory compliance under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013;
(c) Approving the Authority Matrix, financial budgets, and audit reports;
(d) Final escalation of safeguarding, legal, and financial issues;
(e) Board composition and membership admission (Article II of e-AOA).
3.2 Delegated Operational Execution (Executive Officers). Pursuant to Article I and Article 28 of the e-AOA, the Board delegates day-to-day operational execution of the Network to appointed "Executive Officers".
(a) The Executive Officers include the Chief Executive Officer (CEO), Chief Technology Officer (CTO), Chief Operating Officer (COO), Chief Creative Officer (CCO), Chief Financial Officer (CFO), and other heads of operational domains.
(b) Executive Officers are operational leaders. In alignment with the youth-led character of the bits&bytes Network, Executive Officers may be youth leaders or minors, where lawfully permitted and subject to Board-approved safeguards.
(c) Appointment as an Executive Officer is operational and does not confer directorship, membership, statutory signing authority, banking access, or fiduciary authority, except to the extent explicitly delegated in writing by the Board in the Authority Matrix.

3.4 Local Leadership (Forks).
(a) Fork Leads: Appointed individuals responsible for local coordination, safety compliance, and execution of Fork activities.
(b) Local Maintainers: Appointed individuals supporting specific technical, community, or event-based functions within a Fork.
(c) Operational Status: Fork Leads and Local Maintainers exercise local, non-legal, operational coordination. They do not have authority to contract, collect money independently, or represent the legal entity GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION to third parties, unless specifically authorised in writing by the Board.
3.5 Membership of the Company vs. Network Participation.
(a) Formal Members: Members of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION are the subscribers to the e-MOA (Sanjay Singh and Vijay Prakash Singh Kushwaha) and such other persons as the Board formally admits to membership under Article II of the e-AOA. Members are the legal guarantors of the Company (limited to ₹10,000 each) and hold voting rights at general meetings.
(b) Network Participants: Fork Leads, Local Maintainers, contributors, volunteers, and event attendees are network participants. Participation in the Network does not confer membership in GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION, voting rights in the company's statutory affairs, or ownership of the company's assets.
4. Brand, Intellectual Property and Digital Systems
4.1 Foundation Ownership. GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION is the sole legal owner and steward of all intellectual property, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, domain names (including gobitsnbytes.org), code repositories, curriculum resources, design systems, and visual marks associated with the "bits&bytes" brand.
4.2 Contributed Work. All code, design, content, curriculum, documentation, or other assets developed by Executive Officers, Fork Leads, Local Maintainers, volunteers, or participants in connection with the Foundation's programmes or events shall vest in or be licensed to GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION to ensure project continuity and the open-source mission of the Network, subject to any separate agreements approved by the Board.
4.3 Open-Source Licensing. The Foundation defaults to open-source software and open-content licensing (such as MIT, Apache 2.0, or Creative Commons) for educational and public-benefit assets, in alignment with e-MOA Clause 3(a) Object E (Research and Publications) and Object K (Digital Infrastructure). The Board reserves the right to define the licensing terms of any Foundation-funded or Foundation-stewarded project.
4.4 Brand Use Guidelines. No Fork, partner, school, or volunteer may use the "bits&bytes" brand in a manner that:
(a) Implies legal partnership, commercial franchise, or authorization to raise funds independently;
(b) Conflicts with the non-profit status of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION;
(c) Brings the reputation, safeguarding standards, or legal standing of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION into disrepute.
4.5 Digital Systems Administration. Access and administration rights to official domains, social media handles, communication platforms (such as Slack or Discord), cloud hosting, and GitHub organizations are delegated privileges. The Board and the CTO (Akshat Kushwaha) reserve the right to monitor, restrict, transfer, or revoke access at any time for security, compliance, or transition purposes.
5. Legal and Financial Constraints (Section 8 Compliance)
5.1 Application of Income. Under Clause 5 of the e-MOA, all profits, income, and property of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION must be applied solely to the promotion of the objects set forth in the e-MOA. No portion may be paid or transferred, directly or indirectly, by way of dividend, bonus, or profit-sharing to members, directors, or representatives.
5.2 Winding Up. In the event of winding up or dissolution of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION, any remaining assets after satisfying all debts and liabilities shall not be distributed to members. Instead, they must be transferred to another Section 8 company having similar objects, or sold and credited to the Rehabilitation and Insolvency Fund (e-MOA Clause 10).
5.3 Amalgamation. The Company can be amalgamated only with another company registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, and having similar objects (e-MOA Clause 11).
5.4 Regulatory Alterations. No alterations may be made to the e-MOA or e-AOA of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION without the prior approval of the Registrar of Companies (e-MOA Clause 6).
6. Adoption and Amendment
6.1 Binding Nature. This Charter is binding upon all Executive Officers, Fork Leads, Local Maintainers, volunteers, and participants of the bits&bytes Network. Active participation in the Network constitutes acceptance of this Charter.
6.2 Amendments. This Charter may be amended, replaced, or withdrawn by the Board of Directors of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION by way of a Board Resolution.
