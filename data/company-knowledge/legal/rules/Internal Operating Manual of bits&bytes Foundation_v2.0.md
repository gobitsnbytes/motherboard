# Internal Operating Manual of bits&bytes Foundation_v2.0

Internal Operating Manual of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION
Version 2.0 | Effective Date: 4 June 2026 | Approved by the Board of Directors
Version: 2.0 | Effective Date: 4 June 2026 | Approved by: Board of Directors
1. Status, Purpose and Binding Effect
1.1 Legal Status. This Internal Operating Manual ("Manual") is a binding internal rulebook of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION (operating under the public brand name "bits&bytes" or "bitsnbytes", and referred to herein as "the Foundation"), a Section 8 non-profit company incorporated under the Indian Companies Act, 2013, limited by guarantee and not having share capital in the State of Uttar Pradesh, India.
1.2 Constitutional Grounding. This Manual is adopted by the Board of Directors of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION in accordance with the powers granted under the Company's e-Articles of Association (e-AOA) - specifically Articles 19 and 23. This Manual is subordinate to the Company's e-Memorandum of Association (e-MOA), the e-AOA, and the Network Governance Charter (NGC). If there is any inconsistency, the higher-level constitutional documents shall prevail.
1.3 Scope and Purpose. This Manual governs the day-to-day operations of the bits&bytes Network (the "Network"). It applies to all Fork Leads, Local Maintainers, Executive Officers, volunteers, contributors, mentors, and general participants. The purpose of this Manual is to allow high-agency, youth-led building and experimentation while protecting young builders, adult supporters, the Foundation's legal standing, its finances, its brand, its digital infrastructure and its community safety standards.
1.4 Priority over Informal Practice. This Manual prevails over any informal practice, local understanding, chat message, social media description, event listing, badge, title, community habit or local custom that is inconsistent with it.

3. Youth-Led Operations & Minors
3.1 Youth Leadership Mandate. The Foundation is structurally designed to support youth leadership. Minors and young participants may act as contributors, organisers, Fork Leads, Local Maintainers, Executive Officers, and volunteers to the extent permitted by law.
3.2 Protection of Minor Organisers.
(a) Participation by a minor in any leadership, coordination, or executive role is treated as "protected operational participation."
(b) Such participation does not, by itself, impose adult legal responsibility, fiduciary status, personal liability, or statutory signing authority on the minor.
(c) A minor shall not be held personally responsible for adult legal or financial obligations of the Foundation merely because they led a project, coordinated a Fork, spoke publicly, organized volunteers, or communicated with third parties.
3.3 Legal and Financial Limits of Minors.
(a) No minor may independently bind the Foundation, sign contracts, operate corporate bank accounts, accept legal obligations, or make representations of legal authority.
(b) Any operational action by a minor involving financial routing or institutional commitments must be supervised by the Board or an authorised adult officer, and must fit within the written boundaries of the Authority Matrix.
3.4 Titles. Titles used within the Network (such as CEO, CTO, Fork Lead, Local Maintainer, Organizer, or Mentor) describe operational roles and responsibilities only. They do not imply statutory directorship, ownership of corporate assets, employment rights, partnership, or general agency.
4. Safeguarding & Participant Wellbeing
4.1 Safeguarding Priority. Grounded in e-MOA Clause 3(b)(18), safeguarding, participant wellbeing, child protection, harassment prevention, event safety, and online moderation are core operational priorities of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION.
4.2 Scope of Safeguarding. Every Fork, event, online community, repository, group chat, workshop, and cohort program must comply with the Foundation's safeguarding standards.
4.3 Child Protection and Consent. For all activities involving minors, organizers must obtain verified parent/guardian consent, utilize safe venues, ensure appropriate adult supervision ratios, and follow the Foundation's guidelines on communication and data access.
4.4 Code of Conduct. Harassment, bullying, intimidation, stalking, grooming, coercion, retaliation, sexual misconduct, discriminatory conduct, or exclusionary behavior is strictly prohibited across all online platforms, repositories, and physical spaces affiliated with the Network.
4.5 Online Moderation. Official digital communities must be actively moderated. Moderators are authorized to restrict access, delete non-compliant content, issue warnings, and suspend participants who violate safety standards. Material safety incidents must be escalated immediately to the designated Upstream safeguarding contact.
4.6 Emergency Measures. The Board, the CEO (Yash Vardhan Singh), or the CTO (Akshat Kushwaha) may immediately suspend any event, digital channel, repository access, or Fork Recognition to prevent legal, financial, or physical harm to participants or the Foundation.
5. Strict Financial Controls
5.1 Centralised Financial Routing. In accordance with e-MOA Clause 5 (profits applied solely to objects) and e-AOA Table H, all money, sponsorships, grants, donations, event fees, and financial contributions connected with the bits&bytes Network must be routed exclusively through GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION's approved corporate bank accounts and payment systems.
5.2 Absolute Prohibition on Informal Money Flows. The following methods of collecting, holding, or routing funds are strictly prohibited for any activities associated with the brand name "bits&bytes" or any Fork:
(a) Collecting money into personal UPI IDs, personal bank accounts, or personal QR codes;
(b) Cash collections of any kind without written authorization;
(c) Using personal digital wallets (Paytm, PhonePe, GPay, etc.) to hold or pass through corporate funds;
(d) Unapproved fundraising or crowdfunding campaigns;
(e) Informal sponsor collection or side agreements.
5.3 Zero Financial Independence of Forks. Forks do not possess independent financial status. All funds raised in the name of a Fork are legal property of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION and must be routed upstream. Local bank accounts or payment links in a Fork's name are prohibited unless explicitly approved by the Board as part of a formal, audited system.
5.4 Expenses and Reimbursement. No participant or officer may incur expenses or promise reimbursement on behalf of the Foundation unless the expense is within a Board-approved budget, project allotment, or the specific limits of the Authority Matrix. All reimbursement requests must be backed by valid receipts, tax invoices, and operational approvals.

6. The Authority Matrix
6.1 Board Reserves. All statutory, legal, and financial authority belongs to the Board of Directors of GOBITSNBYTES FOUNDATION. No commitment is legally binding on the Company unless executed by an authorised signatory designated in a formal Board Resolution.
6.2 No Implied or Apparent Authority. No person possesses the authority to bind, contract for, spend for, or represent the Foundation merely by virtue of:
(a) Founder status or history;
(b) An operational title (including CEO, CTO, CFO, COO, CCO, Fork Lead, or Maintainer);
(c) Administration access to GitHub, Discord, Slack, domains, or social media;
(d) Possession of an @gobitsnbytes.org email address.
6.3 Authority Matrix Categories:
Contracts and MoUs: Board Resolution required. Execution only by statutory Directors (Sanjay Singh / Vijay Prakash Singh Kushwaha).
Sponsorships and Grants: CFO (Devaansh Pathak) and CEO (Yash Vardhan Singh) may negotiate; final sign-off rests with the Board.
Banking and Payments: Access and management limited to statutory Directors and authorized CFO delegates.
Fork Recognition & Revocation: Reserved exclusively to the Board.
Event Approvals: Required for any event involving third parties, venue agreements, sponsorships, or minors. Approved by CEO (Yash Vardhan Singh) and Head of Operations (Raghwender Vasisth), with safeguarding clearance.
Technology Infrastructure: Domain registration, Supabase production environments, and core GitHub configurations managed by CTO (Akshat Kushwaha) subject to Board oversight.
7. Fork Operating Rules
7.1 Operational Nature. A Fork is an operational abstraction. It exists to facilitate youth-led execution and community coordination. It cannot enter into contracts, sue or be sued, incorporate under its own name, or claim ownership over bits&bytes intellectual property.
7.2 Local Records. Fork Leads must maintain and share basic operational logs:
(a) Current roster of organizers, maintainers, and volunteers;
(b) Records of local events, venues, and attendee counts;
(c) Local partner communications and sponsorships;
(d) Basic incident reports.
7.3 Suspension and Disaffiliation. The Board reserves the right to suspend or disaffiliate a Fork if the Fork operates outside its approved scope, violates safeguarding rules, routes funds informally, or fails to communicate with Upstream.
8. Practical Escalation Rules
8.1 Principle of Early Escalation. When in doubt, team members must escalate to Upstream. Issues involving legal threats, regulatory notices, safety concerns, financial discrepancies, data leaks, or trademark violations must be escalated immediately.
8.2 Stop-and-Escalate Scenarios. A Fork Lead, Local Maintainer, or youth Executive must pause and escalate when:
(a) A school, vendor, or sponsor requests a signed contract, MoU, or indemnity;
(b) A sponsor asks to send funds directly to an individual;
(c) A minor's physical safety or data privacy is compromised;
(d) A participant reports harassment or bullying;
(e) An external party claims trademark infringement or misuse of brand;
(f) There is ambiguity regarding whether an activity is covered by the Company's objects.
9. Enforcement and Breach
9.1 Corrective Action. Violation of this Manual may result in:
(a) Formal warning or performance improvement plans;
(b) Temporary suspension of roles or access privileges;
(c) Archive or revocation of Fork Recognition;
(d) Disassociation from the Network;
(e) Referral to legal counsel or statutory authorities if financial fraud or child endangerment is involved.
9.2 Non-Waiver. Failure to enforce any rule in this Manual in a specific instance does not constitute a waiver of the Foundation's right to enforce the rule in the future.
