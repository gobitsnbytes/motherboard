# W1NGZ tarot and astrology poster

## Purpose

Create a 6 ft × 4 ft landscape promotional poster for W1NGZ at Boys' High School & College, Prayagraj. The poster should read clearly from across a room while feeling like an aged celestial tarot card rather than a generic horoscope advertisement.

## Format and production

- Final trim: 72 × 48 inches, landscape, 3:2 aspect ratio.
- Working raster: 10,800 × 7,200 pixels at 150 PPI, suitable for large-format viewing.
- Bleed: 0.5 inch on every edge, represented in the print PDF where supported.
- Safe area: all essential copy and logos at least 2 inches inside the trim edge.
- Deliverables: print-ready PDF, full-resolution PNG, and a smaller JPG preview.
- Colour mode: RGB master. The print vendor may convert to its required CMYK profile.

## Exact copy

Primary mark:

> W1NGZ

Primary tagline:

> YOUR SIGN, YOUR STORY

Supporting line:

> TAROT • ASTROLOGY • SELF-DISCOVERY

Institution line:

> BOYS' HIGH SCHOOL & COLLEGE, PRAYAGRAJ

Heritage line:

> EST. 1861

No event date, venue, call to action, social handle, or claim about W1NGZ services will be invented.

## Visual direction

The final poster uses a narrow aged-ivory print border around a near-black indigo field. A monumental, cropped sun-and-moon armillary collage occupies the right half. Torn-paper edges, engraved rays, constellation marks, a partial zodiac ribbon, and uneven ink texture borrow from the supplied references without copying a single reference composition.

The palette is midnight navy and near-black with antique gold, aged ivory, muted teal, and restrained vermilion accents. Texture should feel printed and handled, but never obscure the headline.

## Layout

1. The W1NGZ logo dominates the upper-left quadrant and is reproduced from the supplied black-and-white artwork without redrawing or AI alteration.
2. The tagline is stacked below it as a large editorial headline: `YOUR SIGN,` followed by `YOUR STORY`.
3. `TAROT / ASTROLOGY` works as a small category label beneath the headline.
4. The cropped celestial object fills the right half and creates tension against the left-aligned type rather than forming a symmetrical zodiac chart.
5. The Boys' High School & College crest and institution name form a restrained dark footer. The crest is reproduced from the supplied image without altering its colours, motto, or proportions.
6. A fine inner rule provides a print-safe boundary while the underlying texture remains intentionally rough.

## Asset handling

- The uploaded celestial and tarot images are style references only.
- The uploaded W1NGZ artwork is the authoritative brand mark.
- The uploaded school crest is the authoritative school mark.
- Generated artwork must not contain either logo or any critical text. Those elements will be composited afterward so spelling and geometry remain exact.
- Low-resolution logos may be enlarged with careful resampling and modest cleanup, without changing their design.

## Quality checks

- Verify the final canvas is exactly 3:2 and corresponds to 72 × 48 inches.
- Check all supplied copy character by character.
- Confirm both logos retain their original proportions and recognizable geometry.
- Inspect at full size for soft edges, compression artifacts, accidental text, and visual seams.
- Inspect a downscaled preview to confirm the reading order remains W1NGZ, tagline, celestial image, school attribution.
- Confirm all essential content remains inside the safe area and no decorative element creates a false trim line.

## Out of scope

- Rebranding W1NGZ or Boys' High School & College.
- Inventing event details, contact information, QR codes, or astrology claims.
- Producing alternate aspect ratios in this pass.
