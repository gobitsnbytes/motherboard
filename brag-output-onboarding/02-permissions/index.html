Permissions

Who can do what, and in which city.

What it isWhy we use itHow it works
What

A permission is one action. A grant gives it to a person or group, for every city or just one.

Grant
Permissionmeetings.write

Why
1

City boundaries

Delhi’s lead can’t change Mumbai by accident.

2

Access expires

Temporary access ends on its own.

3

Explainable

Every “yes” or “no” has a clear reason.

How
  1. 1

    Discord role → group

    e.g. the fork-lead role puts you in “Fork Lead”.

  2. 2

    Groups hold grants

    A grant is a permission plus a scope.

  3. 3

    Global or one city

    Global covers every city. fork:delhi covers only Delhi.

  4. 4

    Missing one? You’ll see why

    The app names the permission you need. Ask an admin.
