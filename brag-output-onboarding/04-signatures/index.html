Why
1

Legally valid

E-signatures under India’s IT Act, 2000.

2

Verified signers

A 6-digit email PIN confirms each person.

3

Tamper-proof

Sealed PDF with a full audit trail.

How
  1. 1

    Upload the document

    PDF or Word. Word files are converted.

  2. 2

    Add signers, place fields

    Name and email for each signer.

  3. 3

    “Finalize & Send Links”

    Each signer gets their own email link.

  4. 4

    Signer enters PIN and signs

    Draw, type or upload a signature.

  5. 5

    Executed & Sealed

    Everyone’s signed. The PDF is sealed.
